\paragraph{Episodic training did not raise 5-shot accuracy.}
Adding $1000$ episodes ($800$ on EuroSAT-MS) of prototypical training to the dense network changes 5-shot accuracy by \num{d:rs:novel_5:densem-dense} points on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:densem-dense} and \num{d:es:novel_5:densem-dense} on EuroSAT-MS \ci{es:novel_5:densem-dense}, and gains \num{d:rs:novel_1:densem-dense} points \ci{rs:novel_1:densem-dense} at 1 shot on RESISC45. The validation sweeps (supplement), down to a rate of $10^{-5}$, find no learning rate that beats stopping after S0 at 5 shots, the criterion that chose the rate; at 1 shot every rate of the RESISC45 sweep gains \num{tune-rs:gain1:min} to \num{tune-rs:gain1:max} points. Among the pruning schedules (supplement) PMP is ahead of P by \num{d:rs:novel_5:PMP-P} points on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:PMP-P} and by \num{d:es:novel_5:PMP-P} on EuroSAT-MS \ci{es:novel_5:PMP-P}. M$\to$P is ahead by \num{d:rs:novel_5:MP-P} on RESISC45 and the probe P$\to$G$\to$P, which updates no weight in the meta stage, by \num{d:rs:novel_5:PGP-P}, whereas P$\to$M is level with P (\num{d:rs:novel_5:PM-P}). The schedules that gain are exactly those whose pruning score uses the meta-gradient term; M$\to$P has one pruning step and P$\to$G$\to$P no weight update, so neither is needed. The gain lifts DACIS only to the level of random choice. Guo et al.~\cite{guo2020bscd} likewise found meta-learning behind fine-tuning on crop-disease and EuroSAT targets; our conclusion holds for our recipe only (frozen BatchNorm, $1000$ episodes, $5$-way $5$-shot).

\paragraph{Budget, time and the compact networks.}
Accuracy falls steadily with the budget (partitions $0$ to $4$): on RESISC45 DACIS drops from \num{resisc45:C_dacis_PMP_50:novel_5}\% at $50\%$ of the parameters to \num{resisc45:C_dacis_PMP_10:novel_5}\% at $10\%$, against a spread of \num{spread:rs:novel_5:PMP} points among scores at $30\%$. Pruning saves less time than operations (Table~\ref{tab:budget}; CPU is single-thread latency at batch size 1, GPU throughput is at batch size 128, accuracy is for DACIS under PMP on the ResNet-18 rows and for Taylor under PMP or P where marked): at $30\%$ the ResNet-18 needs \num{resisc45:A_l1_PMP_30:macs_128} G multiply-accumulates against \num{resisc45:dense_ce:macs_128} G for the dense network, \num{cost:macs-red:30}\% fewer, but its single-thread CPU latency is only \num{cost:cpu-red:30}\% lower\numif{lat:cpu}{ (\num{lat:red:r18_30}\% on a laptop CPU, \num{lat:cpu})}. We did not profile layer by layer; the untouched stem, shortcuts and residual widths, or memory traffic and call overhead at batch size one, could explain the gap. Unpruned compact networks beat every pruned ResNet-18 on the novel-class sets (Fig.~\ref{fig:budget}). Over ten partitions MobileNetV3-Small, with \num{cost:mobilenet_v3_small:params} M parameters and \num{cost:mobilenet_v3_small:macs} G multiply-accumulates, is \num{d:rs:novel_5:mnv3-dacisPMP:abs} points ahead of the $30\%$ ResNet-18 on RESISC45 and \num{d:es:novel_5:mnv3-dacisPMP:abs} on EuroSAT-MS (DACIS under PMP; against Taylor, \num{d:rs:novel_5:mnv3-taylorPMP:abs} and \num{d:es:novel_5:mnv3-taylorPMP:abs})\numif{lat:cpu}{, and runs in \num{lat:mobilenet_v3_small} ms on the laptop CPU against \num{lat:dense} ms for the dense ResNet-18}. EfficientNet-B0 is higher still on RESISC45 and PlantVillage. PlantDoc is the exception: a ResNet-18 kept at $50\%$ is \num{d:pd:plantdoc_5:mnv3-dacisPMP50:abs} points ahead of MobileNetV3-Small there.

\paragraph{The score matters more under severe pruning.}
We repeated the comparison on MobileNetV2 (Table~\ref{tab:mbv2}). At $40\%$ of the parameters Taylor is \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40:abs} points above random choice on RESISC45 \ci{rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40}, in \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40:wins} of \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40:n} partitions, and \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:25:abs} points at $25\%$, where BatchNorm scale falls \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-bn_gamma-random:25:abs} points below it. The Taylor lead grows with severity on both networks: on RESISC45 it is \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:60}, \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40} and \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:25} points at $60$, $40$ and $25\%$ of the MobileNetV2 and \num{d:rs:novel_5:taylor-random:PMP50}, \num{d:rs:novel_5:taylor-random:PMP}, \num{d:rs:novel_5:taylor-random:PMP20} and \num{d:rs:novel_5:taylor-random:PMP10} at $50$, $30$, $20$ and $10\%$ of the ResNet-18 (five partitions except at $30\%$), so these runs do not separate architecture from severity. \num{cmpm:nbonf} of the \num{cmpm:total} MobileNetV2 comparisons with random choice survive a Bonferroni correction, all on \num{cmpm:bonfeo}; on EuroSAT-MS Taylor is ahead by \num{d:es:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:40:abs} and \num{d:es:novel_5:mp-taylor-random:25:abs} points at $40$ and $25\%$, but neither survives. DACIS is ahead of random choice in \num{cmpm:dacis-above} of its \num{cmpm:dacis-total} comparisons and behind it in \num{cmpm:dacis-below} (\num{cmpm:dacis-belowlist}). Pruned to $40\%$ with the Taylor term, a MobileNetV2 reaches \num{resisc45:MP_taylor_P_40:novel_5}\% on RESISC45 with \num{resisc45:MP_taylor_P_40:macs_128} G multiply-accumulates, \num{d:rs:novel_5:mp-taylor-r18taylor:40:abs} points above the Taylor-pruned ResNet-18, which needs \num{cost:rs:mbv240-vs-r18:macs-ratio} times as many; the unpruned MobileNetV3-Small is still ahead of it by \num{d:rs:novel_5:mnv3-mptaylor:40:abs} points.
