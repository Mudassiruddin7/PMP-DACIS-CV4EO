\section{Experimental protocol}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data.}
\emph{NWPU-RESISC45}~\cite{cheng2017resisc} has $31{,}500$ aerial RGB scenes of $256{\times}256$ pixels, $700$ for each of $45$ scene classes from airport and beach to wetland. We use its single-file Hugging Face copy and resize every scene to $128{\times}128$. \emph{EuroSAT-MS}~\cite{helber2019eurosat} has $27{,}000$ Sentinel-2~\cite{drusch2012sentinel2} patches of $64{\times}64$ pixels (a $640$\,m footprint at $10$\,m resolution) with $13$ spectral bands and $10$ land-use classes. Each band is standardised with the mean and standard deviation of the base-class images. To accept $13$ channels the first convolution repeats the ImageNet filters over the bands and scales them by $3/13$; a control network sees only the visible bands B4, B3 and B2 through the unmodified filters. \emph{PlantVillage}~\cite{hughes2015plantvillage} supplies $54{,}305$ colour photographs of single leaves on a plain background, $38$ classes over $14$ crops, resized to $128{\times}128$. \emph{PlantDoc}~\cite{singh2020plantdoc} was assembled from internet photographs, so its leaves sit in cluttered scenes. We use the cropped-leaf classification release ($2569$ images, $28$ label names); one label has two images and is dropped, which leaves $27$ classes, and images are resized on the short side and centre-cropped to $128{\times}128$. No PlantDoc image is used for training or pruning.

\paragraph{Splits and episodes.}
A seed fixes a random partition of the classes. RESISC45 gives $25$ base, $5$ validation and $15$ novel classes, EuroSAT-MS $5$ base and $5$ novel, PlantVillage $24$ base, $6$ validation and $8$ novel. Seeds $0$ to $4$ give five different partitions per dataset, and every method sees the same one within a seed. PlantDoc is an evaluation set only: its $27$ classes are used for $5$-way episodes after training on PlantVillage base classes, so the test crosses a domain boundary. Because PlantDoc and PlantVillage share some concepts (tomato early blight, for one), this is a field-image test and not a strict novel-class test. Every table reports $5$-way accuracy over $2000$ episodes with $15$ query images per class and $K\in\{1,5,10\}$ supports. The episodes are drawn once per dataset, seed and $K$ and reused by all methods, which makes the comparisons paired.

\paragraph{Training and measurement.}
Stage S0 attaches a new linear head over the base classes and trains for $10$ epochs (RESISC45), $8$ (EuroSAT-MS) or $6$ (PlantVillage) with SGD (Nesterov momentum $0.9$, learning rate $0.02$, weight decay $5{\cdot}10^{-4}$ on weight tensors, $3\%$ linear warm-up then cosine decay, batch $128$, label smoothing $0.1$). Stage R reuses that head for $2$ epochs at $0.01$ on the two Earth-observation sets and one epoch on PlantVillage; the head is dropped at test time, where a parameter-free cosine prototype classifier is used. Stage M keeps BatchNorm statistics frozen, trains the cosine temperature (initialised to $10$) and the other weights, and uses augmented batches. Stage M runs $1000$ episodes ($800$ on EuroSAT-MS), two episodes per step, at a learning rate picked on the validation classes of the first PlantVillage partition from $\{10^{-4},2.5{\cdot}10^{-4},5{\cdot}10^{-4},10^{-3},2{\cdot}10^{-3}\}$ (it chose \num{tune:best}, the smallest value of the grid); all datasets use that value. The same sweep on the RESISC45 validation classes was run as a check and not used for selection. Calibration for the scores uses $40$ images per base class in batches of $64$, with a fixed cosine temperature of $10$. Training images get a random crop (scale $0.6$ to $1$) and random flips; PlantVillage and RESISC45 images also get brightness and contrast jitter. Mixed precision is on. Apart from that learning rate we tuned nothing, and nothing per method. Off-the-shelf baselines (MobileNetV3-Small, MobileNetV2 and EfficientNet-B0 with torchvision ImageNet weights) get the S0 recipe followed by R. All runs used one \num{hw-gpu}. Parameters and multiply-accumulates (MACs) are counted exactly on the pruned network; latency is measured in fp32 on that GPU (batch $128$) and on \num{hw-cpu} (one thread, batch $1$, median of $60$ runs after warm-up) and is not a statement about embedded hardware. With thirteen bands the dense trunk has $11.21$\,M parameters, and EuroSAT-MS budgets are relative to that. The compact networks use the torchvision feature trunk only.

\paragraph{Reporting.}
Tables give the mean over seeds of the per-seed mean accuracy, with the sample standard deviation over the \num{resisc45:seeds} seeds as a subscript. This spread contains the effect of the class partition and of training noise. It is much larger than the sampling error of $2000$ episodes (at most \num{epse:resisc45} points), so we quote it whenever a difference is small. For a pair of methods we give the mean paired difference in points with a $95\%$ $t$-interval over the per-partition differences, and the number of partitions in which the sign agrees. Partitions, not episodes, are the replicates: episodes from one trained network are not independent draws of the training run.
